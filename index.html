    Why this map?

    1. Pin it. When you see a dead animal on the road, drop a pin right where it is.
    2. Find the hotspots. Deaths keep happening at the same spots. The map shows us where.
    3. Fix those spots. We take the map to our HOA and the cities of Southlake and Grapevine, and ask for fixes like:
      • Small tunnels under the road, with low fences that guide animals into them
      • Small-animal crossing signs so drivers slow down and watch out
      • Slower traffic: speed humps and lower limits
